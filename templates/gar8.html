🪴Jelly Bean Plant (Sedum rubrotinctum)

1. The Jelly Bean Plant (Sedum rubrotinctum), also known as Pork and Beans, is a charming succulent recognized for its small, plump, jelly bean-shaped leaves.
2. These fleshy leaves are typically green but can turn shades of red or bronze when exposed to bright sunlight, giving the plant a vibrant, colorful appearance.
3. Native to Mexico, the jelly bean plant is a favorite among succulent enthusiasts due to its quirky look and low-maintenance nature. It also produces small, yellow, star-shaped flowers in the spring, adding to its ornamental appeal.
