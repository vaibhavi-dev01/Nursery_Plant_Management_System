🪴Sago Palm

1. The Sago Palm (Cycas revoluta) is a popular ornamental plant often found in gardens due to its unique, tropical appearance.
2. Although commonly called a palm, it is actually a cycad, one of the oldest plant species that dates back to prehistoric times.
3. The plant features stiff, glossy, dark green fronds that radiate from a central trunk, giving it a striking and symmetrical shape.
