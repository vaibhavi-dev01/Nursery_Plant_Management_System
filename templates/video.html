📝Plant Care Tips


👉  Water plants regularly, but avoid overwatering to prevent root rot.

👉  Ensure plants receive adequate sunlight according to their needs.

👉  Use well-draining soil to keep roots healthy.

👉  Remove dead or yellowing leaves to encourage new growth.

👉  Fertilize plants periodically for healthy development.

👉  Protect plants from extreme weather conditions.

👉  Keep indoor plants away from direct heat sources or cold drafts.

👉  Prune plants to maintain shape and promote flowering.

👉  Check for pests and treat promptly to avoid infestations.
