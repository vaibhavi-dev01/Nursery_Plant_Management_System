🌱Pudina Plant (Mint)

1.The Pudina plant, also known as Mint, is a fast-growing, aromatic herb that is widely used in Indian kitchens. Pudina is commonly used in chutneys, teas, and drinks like lemon water and buttermilk.
2.Pudina is known for its cooling and digestive properties. It helps with digestion, reduces acidity, and keeps the body cool, especially in summer.
3.This plant is very easy to grow at home. It grows well in pots or garden beds with partial sunlight and regular watering.It’s perfect for cooking, health, and even home remedies.
