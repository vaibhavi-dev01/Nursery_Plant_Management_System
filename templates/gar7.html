🪴Cactus

1. Cactus plants are a type of succulent known for their ability to store water in their thick, fleshy stems, allowing them to survive in some of the harshest desert environments.
2. Native primarily to the Americas, especially in arid regions like the southwestern United States and Mexico, cacti are easily recognized by their spines, which are modified leaves that protect them from herbivores and reduce water loss.
3. They come in a wide variety of shapes and sizes—from tall, columnar species like the saguaro to small, round varieties like the golden barrel cactus.
