🪴Snake Plant (Sansevieria trifasciata)

1. Snake plants prefer well-draining soil and only need to be watered when the soil is completely dry, as overwatering can lead to root rot.
2. They tolerate a wide range of temperatures and humidity levels and can even survive drought conditions.
3. Aside from their aesthetic appeal and low maintenance needs, snake plants are also symbolic of good luck and protective energy in many cultures, making them a popular choice for homes and offices.
